    Backup & restore

    Copy this text somewhere safe. To restore, paste it back and press Restore.

    🔒 Private by design: everything you enter is stored only in this browser on this device. It is never sent to any server. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so use “Copy backup” now and then and keep the text somewhere safe. Don’t use a shared or public computer. Never enter account numbers or passwords here; balances and amounts are all this tool needs.
